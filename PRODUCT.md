# Product

## Register
product

## Users
A personal Windows reader following world/local news and public discussion across one or more monitors. The primary workflow is open a profile, catch up, compare coverage, save a story, optionally request a summary, and visit the original publisher.

## Product Purpose
A fast local-first desktop news workspace that remains useful offline and when individual feeds or AI providers fail. The primary sections are AI, Technology, Stocks and Others, with legacy topic workflows preserved. Reporting and discussion are explicitly distinct. Free-source access is a changing constraint, not a guarantee.

## Brand Personality
Clear, compact, dependable.

## Anti-references
No marketing hero, trading-price theater, animated ticker, glowing text, invented sentiment/bias meter, decorative chart, or card-grid dashboard. Do not imply that related headlines establish verified agreement between sources.

## Design Principles
- Reading outranks decoration.
- Show where information came from and what is unavailable.
- Keep the reader in control of preferences, alerts, and AI disclosure.
- Enforce zero-paid runtime behavior in the host, not just in UI copy.
- Preserve place, selection, and workspace across interruptions.
- Use native conventions instead of invented controls.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard-first operation, visible focus, screen-reader landmarks, non-color-only status, normal-text contrast at least 4.5:1, reduced motion, zoom support, and multilingual text. Default profile starts global English; local region/languages remain user-selected. User approved implementation of the saved plan including its conservative defaults.
